## Why this page

Its SERP shares only 1 result with the head term, which means it is a genuinely
separate audience: people already shopping for an external answering service.
They have the budget and the problem, and they have not considered AI.

The SERP is held by טלקול, קישורית, שידורית, VCall and comparison sites
(expert.co.il, yes-please.co.il). Note that several of those comparison sites
rank by *reviewing* the category — worth studying, and possibly worth being
listed on.

## Important framing note

The earlier plan proposed "מזכירה AI מול מזכירה אנושית". That query returns
generic English articles about AI replacing secretaries — zero Israeli
commercial intent. This page targets the Hebrew category term that real buyers
search instead.

## Outline

**H1** מענה אנושי לעסקים מול מזכירה AI — מה מתאים למי

**H2 מה זה מענה אנושי חיצוני** — describe the category fairly and accurately.
A reader who feels the category was strawmanned stops trusting the page.

**H2 איפה מענה אנושי עדיף** — say it plainly. Complex judgement calls, sensitive
conversations, and clients who insist on a person. A comparison that admits the
other option wins somewhere is the one people believe.

**H2 איפה מזכירה AI עדיפה** — instant answer with no hold queue, nights and
weekends, any language without staffing for it, every call logged, no handover
when someone leaves.

**H2 טבלת השוואה** — availability, languages, cost model, booking into the
calendar, logging, ramp-up time. Include rows where AI does not win.

**H2 מה לשאול לפני שבוחרים** — a short checklist. This is the section that gets
cited and linked.

**H2 השלב הבא** — WhatsApp CTA.

## Rules

- Do not name a specific competitor and disparage them. Compare the *categories*.
- Do not claim cost savings percentages you have not measured.
- Do not claim Koli handles everything a person does. The terms page already
  says triage stays with the clinic; the marketing must match.

## Falsifiability

If it ranks and converts, the substitute-category angle is correct and deserves
more pages. If it ranks and does not convert, these buyers want a human and the
cluster should not be expanded.
