## Why this page first

It shares 5 top-10 results with the head term — the tightest measured
relationship in the set. The same vendors that rank for "מזכירה וירטואלית AI"
rank for the cost query, so the page reinforces the homepage rather than
competing with it.

This reverses my earlier advice to skip a pricing page. That advice assumed
there was nothing to say without fixed prices. The SERP shows otherwise:
competitors publish ranges, and the human-secretary comparison does the heavy
lifting.

## Outline

**H1** כמה עולה מזכירה וירטואלית AI

**H2 התשובה הקצרה** — a range and what sits at each end. Say plainly that the
exact figure depends on volume, and that the intro call produces a quote. Do not
invent a number you will not honour.

**H2 מול העלות של מזכירה אנושית** — the strongest section. Salary plus
סוציאליות, sick days, holidays, handover during leave, and the hours nobody
covers. This is where the reader converts.

**H2 מה משפיע על המחיר** — call volume, languages, integrations, how many
actions Koli performs (booking vs message-taking), phone plus WhatsApp.

**H2 מה כלול ומה לא** — set expectations honestly. Name what is not included.

**H3 למה אין מחירון קבוע** — one honest paragraph. A clinic taking 40 calls a day
is not a law office taking 5. This reads better than a fake tier table.

**H2 השלב הבא** — WhatsApp CTA.

## Differentiation

Most competing pages either hide pricing entirely or show a tier table with no
explanation. A page that explains the cost drivers honestly, and compares
against a real salary figure, beats both — and it filters out prospects who were
never going to pay.

## Falsifiability

If this page ranks but produces no WhatsApp clicks (now tracked as
`whatsapp_click` with location `pricing`), the range is scaring people off and
the framing needs work, not more traffic.
