## Why this page

Sibling to `/guides/ai-voice-agent` within the same SERP neighbourhood. Where
that page explains what a voice agent *is*, this one is practical: what to check
before putting one on your business line.

Lowest priority of the four. Build it only after the first three are live and
the cluster shows signs of working.

## Outline

**H1** בוט קולי בעברית — מה באמת עובד

**H2 מה לבדוק לפני שמחברים לקו** — a checklist, which is the real value here:
does it interrupt naturally, does it survive someone switching to Russian
mid-sentence, does it spell Israeli names correctly, what happens when it does
not understand, where do the recordings go.

**H2 הטעויות הנפוצות** — menu trees dressed up as AI, a bot that cannot hand off,
no logging, a voice that sounds robotic in Hebrew even when the English demo was
convincing.

**H2 מה שונה בעברית** — RTL, niqqud-free ambiguity, mixed-script speech
("קבעתי appointment"), numbers and dates spoken in Hebrew word order.

**H2 איך בודקים בעצמכם** — tell the reader to call the vendor's demo line and
try three things: interrupt mid-sentence, switch language, describe something
urgent. Inviting readers to test Koli the same way is a strong trust move —
provided Koli passes.

**H2 השלב הבא** — CTA.

## Rules

- The self-test section only works if Koli genuinely handles all three. Verify
  before publishing, or cut the section.
- Do not name competitors as examples of the common mistakes.
